Accessibility Statement

WAVE22labs wants everyone to be able to use wave22labs.com. This statement explains how we approach accessibility and how to reach us if something doesn’t work for you.

01Our commitment

Our products turn speech into text so that more people can follow what is being said. We hold our own website to the same standard: everyone, including people with disabilities, should be able to find information about WAVE22labs and contact us easily.

02Accessibility level

We aim for wave22labs.com to conform to Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0 at level AA, in line with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013.

[CONFIRM THE LEVEL ACHIEVED AFTER AN ACCESSIBILITY AUDIT OF THE LIVE SITE.]

03What we have done

  • A clear page structure with headings, landmarks (header, navigation, main content and footer) and a logical reading order.
  • Text alternatives for the logo and other meaningful images. Animated diagrams are decorative, hidden from screen readers and explained in the surrounding text.
  • Animations stop when your device’s “reduce motion” setting is on.
  • High-contrast text on light and dark backgrounds, and layouts that work with browser zoom.
  • Hebrew examples are marked with their language and right-to-left direction, so screen readers read them correctly.
  • Links and buttons that can be reached and used with a keyboard. [VERIFY ON THE LIVE SITE]
