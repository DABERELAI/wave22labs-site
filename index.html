02 — Technology

One voice core. Two engines.

Speech recognition and WAVE22labs SmartCorrection sit at the heart of everything we build — delivered online for dictation, and as a closed system for live events.

Engine 01 · DaberelAI · Dictation↗
Your voice in. Clean text out.

Computer mic → Cloudflare → our secure GPU server, where STT and SmartCorrection turn speech into clean text → back to your screen. The text is not stored.

Explore the DaberelAI engine →
Engine 02 · DabereLIVE · Beta↗
From the stage to the screen, live.

Stage mics → control-room mixer → our STT and SmartCorrection → caption engine → the live screen, word by word. One closed system, unaffected by the internet or cellular load.

03 — Privacy by design

Built for sensitive speech.

How our engines handle your voice is part of the architecture, not a setting.

Read our Privacy Policy →

Text is not stored

DaberelAI sends your transcript straight back to your screen. The text is not stored.

Routed through Cloudflare

Your voice travels over Cloudflare’s network to our server — and the text comes back the same way.

Secure GPU server

Speech recognition and SmartCorrection run together on our secure GPU server in the cloud.

Closed system for live events

DabereLIVE runs as its own closed system — from the stage mics to the screen.
