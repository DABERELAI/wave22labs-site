Privacy Policy

How WAVE22labs collects, uses and protects personal information on wave22labs.com and across its products. לגרסה בעברית

01Who we are

WAVE22 LABS LTD. (“WAVE22labs”, “we”, “us”), company no. 515953354, 39 La Guardia St., Tel Aviv-Yafo, Israel, operates wave22labs.com and develops speech and language products (together, the “Services”). We are the data controller. Questions: hello@wave22labs.com.

02Product-specific policies

Each product explains its own data flows in detail:

  • DaberelAI (voice typing for Windows): daberelai.com/privacy. Cloud transcription runs on Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany. Audio and text are processed in memory and are not stored on the server.
  • DabereLIVE and DabereLIVE Production (live captions and transcription studio): these run entirely on the operator’s own computer. Audio, transcripts and exports stay on that computer and are not sent to WAVE22labs. Working files are deleted automatically after 7 days by default. If the operator connects Google Docs, see DabereLIVE and Google Docs: privacy.

Where a product policy and this policy differ, the product policy applies to that product.

03This website

  • No forms, accounts or cookies. wave22labs.com does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web server, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the site.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your name, email and message only to reply and to follow up on what you asked for.

04How we use information

  • To provide the Services, including turning your speech into text and captions.
  • To operate accounts, verify users, provide support and prevent abuse.
  • To maintain and secure the Services.
  • To communicate with you about the Services and updates you asked for.

We do not sell personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your audio or transcripts to train AI models.

05Service providers and transfers

We use Cloudflare (network, security, hosting), Amazon Web Services (cloud transcription, Frankfurt), Vercel (hosting of daberelai.com) and Google (fonts; Google Docs only when a DabereLIVE operator connects it). They process data on our behalf under contracts that protect it. Information may therefore be processed in Israel, the European Union and other countries where these providers operate. Transfers to the EU are permitted under Israel’s Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, and Israel holds an EU adequacy decision. We disclose information to others only when the law requires it.

06Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Emails are kept as long as the conversation is relevant. Product-specific periods are listed in each product’s policy.

07Security

We use encryption in transit, access restrictions and the other measures required by Israel’s Privacy Protection (Data Security) Regulations, 2017. No system is completely secure.

08Your rights

Under Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you may access information about you and ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or out of date. Where the EU GDPR applies, you also have the rights to erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. Write to hello@wave22labs.com. You may also complain to Israel’s Privacy Protection Authority or to your EU supervisory authority.

09Children

The Services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the current version. We will announce material changes through the Services or by email.

11DabereLIVE and Google Docs

If you connect DabereLIVE to your Google account to stream a transcript into Google Docs, see DabereLIVE and Google Docs: privacy for exactly what is accessed, where it goes and how to revoke it.
