Engine 02 · DabereLIVE · Live captions

From the stage
to the screen, live.

DabereLIVE is a closed captioning system for live events. Every microphone on stage feeds the control room mixer, our system turns the sound into clean Hebrew text, and the caption engine puts it on the screen — word by word, as it’s spoken. The system isn’t affected by the internet or by cellular network load.

  • Closed system
  • Unaffected by internet & cellular load
  • Hebrew captions
  • Word by word
  1. 01

    Stage

    Every speaker on stage, on their own microphone.

  2. 02

    Control room

    The mixer combines all channels into one clean feed.

  3. 03

    Our system

    STT and SmartCorrection turn live sound into clean Hebrew text.

  4. 04

    Caption engine

    Formats the text into readable caption lines and timing.

  5. 05

    Live screen

    Words appear as they’re spoken, then settle in place.
