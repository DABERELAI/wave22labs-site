Engine 01 · DaberelAI · Dictation

Your voice in.
Clean text out.

Speak into your computer and DaberelAI types for you. Your voice travels through Cloudflare's secure network to our GPU server, where STT and WAVE22labs SmartCorrection turn it into clean, ordered text — typed straight back onto your screen.

  • Hebrew-first
  • Real-time
  • Text is not stored
  1. 01

    You speak

    Press the hotkey and talk naturally — in Hebrew, English or both.

  2. 02

    Cloudflare

    Your voice streams through a secure network to our servers.

  3. 03

    Secure Server

    STT turns speech into letters; SmartCorrection puts them in order — terms, numbers and mixed Hebrew-English.

  4. 04

    Your screen

    Clean text is typed into the app you’re using. The text is not stored.
